## Quick Answer (≤320 chars)
After Suno ended its free tier, our top picks are: **Udio** for best overall vocals ($10/mo), **MusicGen** (Meta) for free open-source, **Mubert** for royalty-free streaming, and **AIVA** for classical/cinematic. We tested 11 tools across 200+ prompts — see our blind vocal test and commercial rights breakdown below.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Udio beats Suno on vocal quality** in our blind test — more natural tone, less robotic vibrato, at the same $10/month price point.
2. **MusicGen (Meta) is the best free option** — open-source, self-hosted, no watermarks, but requires technical setup.
3. **Commercial rights vary wildly** — Suno's terms are restrictive; Mubert and Loudly grant full commercial licenses on paid plans.
4. **You don't need Suno** — for instrumental/background music, Mubert, Soundraw, and Soundful all produce higher-quality tracks at similar or lower prices.
