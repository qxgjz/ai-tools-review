# 邮件Outreach模板

> 发给已收录工具站站长，交换dofollow链接。
> 每天最多10封，每封必须个性化。
>
> **【补充规则 2026-09-19】** 等 /about 页面上线后，每封邮件正文加一句：
> "We're an independent review team — everything on our site is hands-on testing, no paid placements."
> 并在签名附 /about 链接（https://www.aitoolcrux.com/about），提高回复率。
> /about 未上线前暂不加这句，避免指向不存在的页面。

---

## 模板1：首次接触（给我们已评测的工具站）

**Subject:** We reviewed [Tool Name] — featured on AIToolCrux

Hi [First Name],

I'm [Name] from AIToolCrux — an independent AI tool review site where we hands-on test and compare 500+ AI tools.

We recently published our detailed review of [Tool Name]:
👉 https://www.aitoolcrux.com/tools/[slug]

In our testing, we found [specific positive point — e.g., "your prompt library feature saves real time for developers"]. You can see the full breakdown, screenshots, and performance data in the review.

**Here's a quick idea for a mutual win:**

We'd love to feature [Tool Name] on our homepage as one of our top recommended tools (we rotate 5 featured spots weekly). In return, would you be open to mentioning AIToolCrux on your blog, resources page, or "As Featured In" section? Something as simple as:

> "As featured on [AIToolCrux — Independent AI Tool Reviews](https://www.aitoolcrux.com)"

This works well because:
- We've already given you a detailed, honest review (no paid placement)
- Our audience is developers and researchers actively looking for AI tools
- It's a genuine recommendation, not a paid link

Would you be open to this? Happy to send our review badge or more details.

Best,
[Name]
AIToolCrux Team
https://www.aitoolcrux.com

## 个性化检查清单（每封邮件发送前）

- [ ] 提到对方工具的具体名称
- [ ] 提到一个我们评测里的具体优点（不要模板化）
- [ ] 附上我们评测页的具体URL
- [ ] 交换条件清晰（首页推荐位）
- [ ] 没有群发痕迹
- [ ] 签名完整

## 发送节奏
- 每天最多10封
- 间隔至少30分钟
- 周一至周四发送（周五至周日回复率低）
- 发送时间：北京时间晚上8-10点（对方早上8-10点）
