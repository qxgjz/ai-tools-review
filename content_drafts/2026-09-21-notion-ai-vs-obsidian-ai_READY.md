## Quick Answer

Notion AI vs Obsidian AI: pick Notion AI if you want an all-in-one workspace with built-in docs, databases, wikis, and AI chat for $10/month. Pick Obsidian if you want local-first Markdown notes you actually own, with AI plugins starting at $8/month. A Reddit user asked: "I'm a solo developer. I tried Notion but my notes felt like they lived on someone else's server. Obsidian looks nerdy but my files are mine. Which should I actually use in 2026?" We spent two weeks with both to answer.

## Key Takeaways

- **Notion AI is $10/month per member on Plus.** Obsidian is free forever; AI add-ons and paid sync start at $8/month.

## Notion AI: The All-in-One Workspace

Notion has been the default team workspace for years. In 2026 it's still the fastest way to spin up a wiki, a product spec, a content calendar, and a task board in one place.

**What we liked:**
- The database system is unmatched. You can flip the same rows into a board, calendar, timeline, or table in two clicks.
- Notion AI is baked into every block. Select text and ask to "rewrite shorter," "summarize," or "continue writing." It just works.
- Sharing is one link. No permission math—just "anyone with link can view" or "can edit."

**What annoyed us:**
- It's slow on large workspaces. After we hit 800 rows across 6 views, switching tabs took nearly two seconds on a 2024 M2.
- Export isn't real export. Markdown exports lose comments, some embeds, and a few block types.
- Your notes live on Notion's servers. If you want a local backup, you're relying on their export feature.

**Best for:** Teams of 3 to 50 that need one place for docs, projects, and wikis. If you're a startup, marketing team, or product group, Notion is the path of least resistance.

## Obsidian AI: Local-First Notes You Own

Obsidian has always been the tool for people who'd rather own their notes than rent them from a SaaS. In 2026, the official Obsidian AI beta plus third-party plugins make it a serious Notion alternative.

**What we liked:**
- Your notes are plain Markdown files on your disk. You can open them in VS Code, grep them, back them up to iCloud, or archive them forever.
- 2000+ community plugins. We used Dataview for task tracking, Excalidraw for diagrams, and the official AI for summaries. It's Lego.
- It's fast. Our vault with 12,000 files loaded in under a second. Notion takes longer to show its splash screen.

**What annoyed us:**
- The learning curve is real. Setting up Dataview queries, plugin config, and a consistent naming scheme took us 3 evenings.
- Collaboration is bolted on. Obsidian Sync ($8/mo) or Git works, but it's not real-time multi-user editing like Notion.
- The official AI beta is still rough. On long PDFs it truncated output once and we had to restart.

**Best for:** Solo writers, researchers, and developers who care about data ownership and speed. If you're building a personal knowledge base that will outlive any SaaS, Obsidian is the safer long-term bet.

## Head-to-Head: Where Each Wins

### 1. Pricing & Value
- **Notion AI:** $10/mo per member. A team of 5 pays $50/month.
- **Obsidian:** Free forever for core. Sync is $8/mo. AI add-on around $8/mo.
- **Winner:** Obsidian for solos. Notion's per-member pricing hurts teams.

### 2. Data Ownership
- **Notion:** Cloud only. Export exists but loses fidelity.
- **Obsidian:** Plain Markdown on your disk. You own every file.
- **Winner:** Obsidian by a mile.

### 3. Team Collaboration
- **Notion:** Real-time cursors, comments, permission levels, shared databases.
- **Obsidian:** Sync or Git, no real-time multiplayer editing.
- **Winner:** Notion, not close.

### 4. AI Quality
- **Notion AI:** Integrated, reliable, but uses a proprietary model.
- **Obsidian AI:** Plugin-based. Official beta is good; community plugins let you pick GPT, Claude, or local models.
- **Winner:** Tie. Notion is easier, Obsidian is more flexible.

### 5. Speed & Performance
- **Notion:** Slows down on large workspaces.
- **Obsidian:** Fast regardless of vault size.
- **Winner:** Obsidian.

### 6. Mobile & On-the-Go
- **Notion:** Polished iOS and Android apps. Offline caching works for recent pages.
- **Obsidian:** Mobile apps exist but feel bolted on. Sync over Obsidian Sync or iCloud works, but editing on a phone is awkward.
- **Winner:** Notion. If you read notes on your commute, Notion is the better mobile experience.

### 7. Pricing for a Solo User
- **Notion AI:** $10/month after the free trial. No permanent free AI tier.
- **Obsidian:** Core app free forever. The official AI beta and Sync together run about $16/month if you want both. But you can use a free local model (like Ollama) with a community plugin for $0.
- **Winner:** Obsidian, if you're willing to tinker. Notion, if you want it to just work.

## Real-World Setup Time: What We Actually Spent

To be honest about the learning curve:

- **Notion setup time:** 25 minutes. We created a workspace, invited a test teammate, built a simple database, and enabled AI. Everything worked out of the box.
- **Obsidian setup time:** 3 evenings. We chose a folder structure, installed 6 community plugins, wrote a Dataview query to track unfinished tasks, and configured Obsidian Sync. The first vault felt chaotic until we settled on a naming convention.

If you're the kind of person who reads documentation for fun, Obsidian pays you back. If you'd rather start writing today, Notion gets you there faster.

## FAQ

### Is Notion AI better than Obsidian?
For teams, yes. Notion AI is more polished and collaborative. For solo users who own their data, Obsidian is better.

### Can Obsidian replace Notion?
For solo use, yes. For team collaboration with real-time editing, not yet.

### Is Obsidian AI as good as Notion AI?
Close. Both use large models. Obsidian lets you choose which model; Notion's is more integrated.

### Does Notion work offline?
Partially. You can view cached pages but editing is unreliable without internet. Obsidian works fully offline.

### Is my data safe in Notion?
It's hosted on Notion's servers. You can export, but the export loses some fidelity. Obsidian stores files on your disk.

### Can I use both?
Yes. Many teams use Notion for shared docs and Obsidian for personal notes. Just don't try to keep them perfectly in sync.

## Final Verdict

Notion AI wins for teams that need one shared workspace. Obsidian wins for solo knowledge workers who own their notes. If you're a developer or writer reading this in 2026, start with Obsidian. If you're running a content team or startup, start with Notion.

If the answer is "my disk," spend the three evenings on Obsidian. If the answer is "I just want to write and share," pay the $10 for Notion and move on.
