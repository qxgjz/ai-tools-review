# Notion AI vs Salesforce Einstein 2026: Which Wins for Your Team?

Notion AI is the artificial intelligence layer inside Notion, a workspace that combines docs, wikis, databases and project management in one tool. Salesforce Einstein is the AI layer inside Salesforce, a customer relationship management (CRM) platform that tracks sales pipelines, leads, accounts and service cases. These two products are not direct rivals in the traditional sense, but teams in 2026 are actively choosing between them because both claim to be "the AI your team works in," and the choice comes down to where your team's work actually lives.

## Quick Answer: Notion AI vs Salesforce Einstein in 2026

**Short version: Notion AI wins for SMB knowledge management and content collaboration, and it is dramatically cheaper to adopt. Salesforce Einstein wins for enterprise sales automation, lead scoring and pipeline forecasting, but only when your data already lives in Salesforce.** Notion AI is included with Notion's Business plan at €19.50 per member/month, with new 2026 capabilities like Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes and Enterprise Search ([Notion AI product page](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/product/ai)). Salesforce Einstein is sold as an add-on: Sales Cloud editions run from $25 to $300 per user/month, and Einstein for Sales adds roughly $75 per user/month on top ([Salesforce add-on pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/platform/pricing-add-ons/)). If you are a 10-person team that lives in docs, start with Notion AI. If you run a revenue organization with a pipeline in Salesforce, Einstein is the AI that understands that pipeline.

## What Changed in 2026: Notion AI Is No Longer an Add-On

The biggest 2026 change is pricing structure. Earlier guides described Notion AI as a $10/user/month bolt-on. That is outdated. In 2026, Notion bundles its AI into the Business and Enterprise plans, with core features like Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes and Enterprise Search included ([Notion pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing)). The plans are:

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | €0 per member/month | Trial AI capabilities, basic forms, basic sites, Notion Calendar |
| Plus | €9.50 per member/month | Everything in Free plus custom forms, unlimited charts, basic connections |
| Business | €19.50 per member/month | Everything in Plus plus Notion Agent, Custom Agents, AI Meeting Notes, Enterprise Search, SAML SSO |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing | Everything in Business plus SCIM provisioning, audit log, zero data retention with LLM providers |

Two 2026 details matter for budgeting. First, Custom Agents (agents that run on schedules or triggers) are free until 3 May 2026, then consume Notion credits priced at $10 per 1,000 monthly credits ([Notion AI FAQ](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/product/ai)). Second, Enterprise Search now reaches across connected apps like Slack, GitHub and Google Drive, which turns Notion into a query layer for the whole company, not just its own pages. For a team that already pays for Notion Business, the AI capability is effectively included in the seat price.

Salesforce, by contrast, still sells AI as a stack of add-ons. The base Sales Cloud editions are Essentials at $25 per user/month, Professional at $75, Enterprise at $150 and Unlimited at $300 per user/month ([Salesforce pricing guide](https://www.salesforce.com/platform/pricing-add-ons/)). On top of that, Einstein for Sales is roughly $75 per user/month, Sales Cloud Einstein is $50 per user/month, Einstein Predictions is $75 per user/month, and Einstein Copilot is bundled only at the Unlimited tier. The honest way to budget an Einstein deployment in 2026 is to take the edition price and add $50-200 per user for the AI pieces you actually want.

## Feature-by-Feature: What Each AI Actually Does

### Document and Knowledge Work

Notion AI excels at generative text tasks inside a workspace. You can ask it to draft a page, summarize a long meeting transcript, rewrite a proposal, brainstorm project names, or pull answers from your connected apps via Enterprise Search. Its 2026 additions push it toward autonomous work: Notion Agent can "complete complex, multi-step tasks using context from Notion, your connected apps and the web," and AI Meeting Notes transcribes and summarizes meetings without a separate bot ([Notion AI](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/product/ai)).

Salesforce Einstein is not built for document generation. It summarizes customer data, drafts email sequences and generates pipeline forecasts, but general-purpose writing is not its job. If your team's core output is documents, wikis and meeting notes, Notion AI is the better fit.

### Sales, Leads and Pipeline

Salesforce Einstein is the deep AI layer for revenue operations. Its flagship capabilities are lead scoring, opportunity scoring, account insights, next best action, and predictive forecasting built on your CRM history ([Salesforce Einstein for Sales](https://www.salesforce.com/platform/pricing-add-ons/)). Einstein Copilot, the conversational assistant, acts on CRM records: it can draft follow-up emails, summarize an account's entire history, and suggest next steps grounded in your actual pipeline data. Notion AI has no equivalent here. It can track basic contacts in a database, but it has no lead-scoring model, no pipeline stages and no forecasting engine.

### Automation and Workflow

Both products added agent-style automation in 2026. Notion's Custom Agents run on schedules or triggers and can route tasks, answer questions in Slack and share project updates, consuming credits after May 2026 ([Notion AI](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/product/ai)). Salesforce's Einstein agents (Einstein Bots for service, Einstein Copilot actions for sales) operate inside the CRM data model, which is a materially different capability: the agent has structured knowledge of your accounts, opportunities and cases. If your automation needs are document-centric, Notion works. If your automation needs to act on CRM records, Einstein is the only one of the two that can.

## When to Choose Notion AI

- Your team lives in docs, wikis, databases and meeting notes.
- You want AI included in a flat seat price with no add-on math.
- You need autonomous agents for document and task workflows (Notion Agent, Custom Agents).
- You are a startup or SMB that does not have a full CRM yet.
- You want Enterprise Search across Slack, GitHub and Google Drive.

## When to Choose Salesforce Einstein

- Your revenue depends on pipeline visibility, lead scoring and forecasting.
- Your team already operates inside Salesforce and needs AI grounded in CRM records.
- You need Einstein Copilot to summarize account histories and draft next steps.
- You run service operations where Einstein Bots and case classification add value.
- Your data volume and sales process justify $150-300 per user per month.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can Notion AI replace Salesforce?**

No. Notion AI is a document and knowledge assistant, not a CRM. It can track contacts in a database but has no pipeline stages, lead scoring or forecasting. For sales operations, Salesforce Einstein (or a dedicated [sales assistant tool](/blog/best-ai-sales-assistants-2026/)) is the appropriate layer.

**Is Notion AI included in Notion plans in 2026?**

Yes, on Business and Enterprise plans. Core features like Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes and Enterprise Search are included at €19.50 per member/month for Business (billed per member). Lower plans get limited trial usage ([Notion pricing](https://www.notion.com/pricing)).

**How much does Salesforce Einstein cost per user in 2026?**

Sales Cloud editions run $25 (Essentials) to $300 (Unlimited) per user/month, and Einstein for Sales adds roughly $75 per user/month ([Salesforce add-on pricing](https://www.salesforce.com/platform/pricing-add-ons/)). Einstein Copilot is included at the Unlimited tier, so the realistic per-user cost for full Einstein is $150-300 per month.

**Which is better for a small team, Notion AI or Salesforce Einstein?**

For a small team, Notion AI. The Business plan includes AI at €19.50 per user/month with near-zero setup. Salesforce Einstein starts at $25 per user/month for the base CRM and needs an add-on for the AI, plus weeks of implementation. Start with Notion unless you already run Salesforce.

**Can Notion AI agents automate CRM-like workflows?**

Notion Custom Agents can route tasks, answer questions and share updates on schedules or triggers, and they consume Notion credits after 3 May 2026 ($10 per 1,000 credits) ([Notion AI FAQ](https://www.notion.com/en-gb/product/ai)). But they operate on workspace data, not on CRM objects, so they cannot replace Einstein's lead scoring or pipeline automation.
