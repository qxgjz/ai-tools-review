## FAQ

### Is there a completely free Claude alternative?
Yes. Llama 3.1 70B runs free on your own machine, and Qwen 2.5 72B offers a free API tier. For browser-based free chat, ChatGPT's free tier and Gemini's free tier both work but have rate limits.

### Which Claude alternative is best for coding?
GitHub Copilot at $10/month. It's native to VS Code and gives inline autocomplete. Claude 3.5 Sonnet is better for architecture discussions, but for day-to-day coding, Copilot wins.

### Which Claude alternative has the longest context window?
Google Gemini Advanced has a 2 million token context window — about 10x larger than Claude's 200K. It can process entire codebases or long research papers in one prompt.

### Is ChatGPT better than Claude in 2026?
For everyday tasks, yes. ChatGPT is faster, has plugins, and a larger ecosystem. For long-form creative writing and nuance, Claude 3.5 Sonnet still wins.

### Can I use Claude alternatives offline?
Yes. Llama 3.1 70B, Qwen 2.5 72B, and Mistral can all run locally on a machine with 16GB+ RAM or a GPU with 24GB VRAM.

### Are Claude alternatives safe for sensitive data?
Local models like Llama are safest because data never leaves your machine. Cloud tools like ChatGPT and Gemini train on your prompts unless you opt out in settings.
