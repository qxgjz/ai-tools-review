## Perplexity vs ChatGPT: Which Is Better for Research?

Research is Perplexity's home turf, and the pricing page reflects it. **Pro at $17/month annual is the research-focused plan**: deep research (multi-step cited investigation), access to top AI models, model selection per query, and 4,000 bonus credits — plus the core promise that citations appear in every answer, which is the feature users actually trust for verification. **ChatGPT Plus** also has deep research and citations, but it is one feature inside a general assistant rather than the product's center — the plan page lists expanded deep research among many expanded items.

## Deep Research: Perplexity vs ChatGPT Side by Side

Deep research is the feature buyers compare most, and the two products implement it differently. **Perplexity Pro's deep research** is the flagship: multi-step, source-grounded investigation that returns a cited report, and the pricing page ties it to the Pro identity — "built for serious work" — alongside 4,000 bonus credits and access to top AI models. **ChatGPT's deep research** is an expanded capability on Plus and maximum on Pro: the model plans a research task, searches, and writes a cited report, but it sits inside the assistant rather than defining it.

## Model Choice: Perplexity's Router vs ChatGPT's Stack

The model strategy is a hidden differentiator. **Perplexity is a model router** — the pricing page explicitly promises "access to top AI models" and "select between AI models," meaning Pro users can pick the best model per question, which is the most flexible way to get accurate answers. **ChatGPT is a model stack** — GPT-6 across the consumer plans, GPT-6 Astra on Pro for reasoning, and Codex for coding; the models are chosen for you per feature, which is simpler and more integrated but less flexible.

The practical effect: Perplexity Pro wins when a question benefits from a specific frontier model or when you want to compare model outputs side by side; ChatGPT wins when you want a single coherent assistant whose models are already wired to the features.
