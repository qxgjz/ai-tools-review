## How to Use ElevenLabs v2 Without Burning Through Credits

The single biggest complaint about ElevenLabs in community threads is credits disappearing fast. Three habits fix that:

1. **Write the script first, then generate once.** Every regeneration costs characters, and regenerating a 30-second clip ten times burns a full free-tier month. Edit the script, finalize it, then generate.
2. **Use the lowest quality model that sounds acceptable** for drafts, and only switch to the flagship model for the final render. Draft quality is for pacing and timing; final quality is for publication.
3. **Export in one pass** and do audio cleanup in your editor instead of regenerating in ElevenLabs. Removing a mispronounced word in Audacity is free; regenerating the whole clip is not.
