## Why Look for an Otter.ai Alternative?

Otter's 2026 price hike and feature caps created real frustration:

1. **The 90-minute cap on Pro.** Enterprise-heavy meetings routinely exceed 90 minutes. Business ($19.99/user/mo) lifts it to 4 hours, but that's a 2.4x price jump for a limit most tools don't have.
2. **The price hike itself.** Users on Reddit r/meetingnotes and r/productivity report surprise auto-renewal increases — exactly the "被自动续费扣钱" pain point we track as P0.
3. **Bot fatigue.** Some users simply don't want an AI joining every call (Zoom fatigue extends to bots). Granola's approach — local notes, no bot — solves that.

## The Accuracy Test: What the Word Error Rates Actually Said

Transcription accuracy is the make-or-break metric, and the differences across tools were smaller than pricing differences — but still meaningful. We measured word error rate on a noisy product demo with two non-native speakers and one fast-talking presenter.

- **Otter.ai:** 94% accurate on clean audio; dropped to ~88% on the noisy call, and struggled with one speaker's accent.
- **Fireflies:** 95% on clean audio; ~89% on the noisy call — similar accuracy, but its speaker identification held up better across crosstalk.
- **Fathom:** 93% on clean audio; ~87% on noise — fine for sales notes, weaker on heavy accents.
- **MeetGeek:** 92% clean / 86% noisy — acceptable for summaries, not for verbatim records.
- **Airgram:** 94% clean / 88% noisy — solid, with good multilingual support.
- **NotebookLM:** 97% on uploaded clean audio — the best we measured, because it processes the full file offline rather than a live stream.

The takeaway: accuracy differences are 1-3 points between the leading tools, so your decision should hinge on meeting caps, price, and workflow fit — not on the claim that one tool "transcribes better" than another.

## What About Privacy and Compliance?

Meeting transcription means your conversations live in a vendor's cloud, and teams in regulated industries care about this:

- **Otter Enterprise** adds SSO, SCIM, domain capture, and a HIPAA compliance add-on — the strongest compliance story in this list, at enterprise pricing.
- **Fireflies** is GDPR and SOC2 compliant, which covers most commercial needs.
- **Granola** is the privacy standout for individuals: notes are processed locally, no bot joins the call, and nothing is transcribed to a cloud vendor unless you export it.
- **NotebookLM** runs on Google infrastructure; enterprise customers can lean on Google's data processing terms.

If your industry requires HIPAA or strict data residency, Otter Enterprise is the safe choice — but expect to negotiate enterprise pricing. For everyone else, Fireflies' SOC2 compliance is sufficient, and Granola is the privacy-first pick for solo users.
