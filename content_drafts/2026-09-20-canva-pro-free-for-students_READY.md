## The 5-Step Official Process (2026)

### Step 1: Go to the Education page, not the pricing page

This is where 90% of students get it wrong. If you go to canva.com/pricing and click "Try Pro free," you land on the **trial flow**, which asks for a credit card. That's not the Education program.

Go directly to: **canva.com/education** (or search "Canva for Education" — it's the first result). You should see a page titled **"Canva for Education"** with a green **"Verify with your school"** button.

**Red flag:** If you see a "Start your 30-day free trial" headline, you're on the wrong page. Back out and go to `/education`.

### Step 2: Sign in or sign up with your school email

Click **"Verify with your school."** You'll be asked to sign in. Use your **school-issued email** — in the US that ends in `.edu`, but many high schools and community colleges use custom domains (e.g., `firstname.lastname@students.highschool.edu`).

If your school uses Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 Education, you may be auto-detected instantly. Otherwise you'll enter your email manually and click **"Send verification."**

### Step 3: Check your school email for the verification link

Canva sends a magic-link email. Click it. If your school domain is on Canva's approved list (most accredited US schools are), approval is **automatic and instant**.

If it's **not** on the list, you'll see a "manual review" screen. This usually happens with:
- Homeschoolers
- Students at smaller international schools
- Recent high-school graduates taking a gap year

For manual review, Canva asks you to upload **proof of enrollment**: a tuition receipt, class schedule, or student ID. It takes 1–3 business days.

### Step 4: Open Canva — Pro is already unlocked

You don't need a promo code. Once approved, sign into any Canva app (web, iOS, Android, desktop) and you'll see **"Canva Pro"** in the top-right. The Brand Kit, Background Remover, Magic Resize, and 100M+ stock library all work.

**No credit card prompt. No "upgrade to continue" modal. No 7-day countdown.** That's the whole point of the Education program.

### Step 5: It lasts 1 year — re-apply before it expires

The Education license lasts **12 months**. About a month before it ends, Canva emails you to re-verify. You re-apply the same way. If you graduate, the license expires at the end of your current 12-month period — it doesn't downgrade mid-year.

## Common Gotchas (We Ran Into These)

**"It says my school isn't eligible."** This happens when your school uses a domain Canva hasn't catalogued. Click the **"Request your school"** link at the bottom of the verification page. Canva usually approves it within a week if the school is accredited.

**"I graduated, can I keep it?"** No. The license is tied to active enrollment. Canva doesn't actively audit, but if you re-apply after graduation with a personal email, you'll be rejected. Don't try to game this.

**"Can I use it for my side hustle?"** Technically the Education license is for **non-commercial educational use only**. Using it to design client deliverables violates the terms. If you're freelancing, get the $12.99/month Pro plan.

**"It's asking for a credit card after all."** You're in the trial flow, not Education. Clear your cookies, go directly to canva.com/education, and start over.

## If You Don't Qualify

Not every student gets approved immediately. Here's what to do if Canva rejects you:

**Homeschoolers:** Canva added homeschool support in 2025 but requires a verification letter from your parent/guardian. Use the "request your school" form and select "homeschool" from the dropdown.

**Gap-year students:** Once you graduate high school and defer college, you technically lose student status. Canva doesn't verify continuously during the 12-month window, but your next renewal will require an active enrollment proof.

**International students:** Canva Education works for students outside the US, but the approved school list is smaller. If your university isn't listed, use the request form — most large universities get approved within a week. You'll need to upload a student ID or tuition bill in PDF format.

**Non-students who still need free Canva:** The free tier (250k templates, 5GB storage) is genuinely usable for basic social graphics. If you need Background Remover or Magic Resize, the $12.99/month Solo plan is cheap enough that the "hunt for free Pro" dance isn't worth the time. Time is money — if you're spending more than an hour trying to find a free Pro hack, you've already lost more than a month of Pro costs.

## FAQ

### Is Canva Pro free for students?
Yes. Accredited students (K-12 and college) get Canva Pro free for 1 year through the Education program. No credit card required.

### Do I need a `.edu` email?
Not always. Many high schools and international schools use custom domains that are already approved. If yours isn't, you can request it.

### How long does approval take?
Instant if your school domain is auto-approved. 1–3 business days if manual review with proof of enrollment.

### Can I use Canva Pro Education for commercial work?
No. The Education license is for non-commercial educational use. Freelancers and businesses should pay for Pro or Teams.

### What happens after 1 year?
You re-apply with proof you're still a student. If you've graduated, the license expires and you move to the free tier.

### Does Canva Education include the AI tools?
Yes. Magic Write, Magic Design, Magic Resize, and Background Remover all unlock.

### Can I use Canva Pro on my phone?
Yes. The Education license works across web, iOS, Android, and the desktop app. Sign in with the same school email.

### What if I already started a free trial?
Cancel it. If you apply for Education while on a trial, Canva may ask you to wait for the trial to end. Don't enter a credit card for the trial — just sign up for Education directly.
