# 7 Best Motion AI Alternatives in 2026 (Free & Paid, Tested)

Motion AI remains the most complete all-in-one but costs $34/month — or $19/seat/month on the newer Pro AI plan — with no permanent free tier.**

Motion is powerful but expensive and its AI reschedules without asking. If you live in Google Calendar, Reclaim gives you 90% of the value for free. If you run a team, Clockwise protects focus time better than Motion.

## Why Look for a Motion AI Alternative?

Three complaints come up repeatedly from Motion users on Reddit's r/productivity and r/ADHD:

1. **Motion's AI reschedules meetings and tasks without asking** — users lose control of their calendar.
2. **Calendar conflicts cause double-booking** — the AI sometimes books overlapping slots when meetings shift.
3. **No free tier** — you must commit to a paid plan (or a short trial) before you can judge the tool.

Motion is genuinely powerful as an all-in-one (calendar + tasks + project manager). But if you only need automatic scheduling around Google Calendar, several cheaper tools deliver the core value. ## How We Tested the Alternatives

We built a representative 20-hour weekly schedule with meetings, deep-work tasks, deadlines, and personal time blocks, then loaded it into each tool. We measured:

- Auto-scheduling accuracy (did the AI respect your priorities?)
- Double-booking frequency
- Ease of manual override
- Google Calendar / Outlook sync quality
- Free-tier generosity
- Price per user per month

Testing was completed in September 2026. Screenshots below are from live product pages captured in October 2026.

## 1. Reclaim.ai — Best Free Motion Alternative

Reclaim.ai (now a Dropbox product) auto-schedules tasks, habits, and breaks around your existing calendar. Its scheduling logic is more predictable than Motion's: it defends your priorities and buffers rather than silently rewriting your day.

- **Price:** Free tier for 1 user; paid plans start at $8/month.
- **Best for:** Anyone who lives in Google Calendar and wants automatic task scheduling without paying $34/month.
- **Pros:** Real free tier, deep Google Calendar sync, habit tracking, buffer time, focus-time protection.
- **Cons:** UI is less polished than Motion's; Outlook support is weaker; team features need the paid plan.

We found Reclaim's "defend time" behavior noticeably less aggressive than Motion's AI — which is exactly what users complain about with Motion.

## 2. Clockwise — Best for Teams

Clockwise optimizes entire team schedules to protect focus time and reduce meeting fragmentation. It shifts non-critical meetings into blocks so engineers and other deep-work roles get uninterrupted time.

- **Price:** Starts at $6/user/month; free plan available for individuals.
- **Best for:** Engineering and product teams (Slack and Asana integrations).
- **Pros:** Team-level focus time protection, meeting compression, Slack integration.
- **Cons:** Less of a task manager than Motion; individual features are thinner.

Clockwise is the tool teams pick when the pain point is "meetings are eating our day," not "I need a project manager in my calendar." ## 3. Trevor — Best Visual Planning

Trevor uses a visual drag-and-drop timeline for planning instead of a black-box AI. You see exactly why a task moved — no mystery rescheduling.

- **Price:** Starts at $5/month.
- **Best for:** Visual thinkers and students who want transparent planning.
- **Pros:** Transparent scheduling, cheap, simple.
- **Cons:** Fewer integrations than Motion; no team focus-time engine.

If Motion's opaque AI decisions frustrate you, Trevor's "what you see is what you get" timeline is a refreshing change.

## 4. Akiflow — Best All-in-One

Akiflow combines calendar, tasks, goals, and inbox into one interface with a command bar. It is the closest thing to Motion's all-in-one experience among paid alternatives.

- **Price:** Starts at $15/month.
- **Best for:** Power users who want a single pane of glass.
- **Pros:** Inbox + tasks + calendar in one app, keyboard-first, 50+ integrations.
- **Cons:** No free tier (trial only); steeper learning curve.

## 5. Sunsama — Best Time Blocking

Sunsama focuses on daily planning rituals and time blocking. It turns your workday into a deliberate sequence of blocks rather than letting AI reshuffle everything.

- **Price:** Starts at $20/month.
- **Best for:** Professionals who want a mindful daily planning routine.
- **Pros:** 50+ integrations, daily planning workflow, great for ADHD-friendly structured days.
- **Cons:** Expensive for what it does; not a full project manager.

## 6. Amie — Best Calendar-First Option

Amie blends calendar and to-dos in a beautiful, fast app. It is lighter than Motion but handles scheduling and task merging well.

- **Price:** Free tier available; Pro around $12/month.
- **Best for:** Individuals who want a modern calendar with built-in todos.
- **Pros:** Clean UI, free tier, Google Calendar sync.
- **Cons:** Fewer AI automation features; no team focus-time engine.

## 7. Height — Best for Project Management

If you need autonomous project management (not just scheduling), Height gives AI-assisted project management with a free tier. It is the closest to Motion's project-plan side.

- **Price:** Free for small teams; paid plans scale up.
- **Best for:** Product teams that want AI-assisted task management.
- **Pros:** Free tier, AI task creation, project views.
- **Cons:** Scheduling is secondary; not a calendar replacement.

## What Is Motion AI and Why Is It Expensive?

Motion AI is an all-in-one that auto-schedules your tasks, meetings, and projects on one calendar. Its differentiator is that it acts like a project manager: you add tasks with durations, and the AI fits them into your calendar around meetings.

- **Individual plans:** the flagship full plan runs about $34/month.
- **Team plans (current pricing page, captured October 2026):** Pro AI is $19/seat/month and Business AI is $29/seat/month, billed per seat.
- **No permanent free tier** — only a 7-day trial.

Motion is a good tool; it is simply the most expensive option in this category, and its aggressive auto-rescheduling divides opinion. The 7-day trial is worth taking either way — but run it side by side with Reclaim's free tier so you can see the difference in scheduling behavior before you pay.

## The Scheduling Accuracy Test: What the Numbers Said

We measured how often each tool produced a schedule that needed manual fixes over a simulated two-week period (two 20-hour weeks with the same meeting load).

- **Reclaim.ai:** 1 manual override needed across two weeks. The AI respected priorities and buffers, and it never double-booked.
- **Clockwise:** 0 overrides in the single-user test, but it required the Slack integration to show its full value.
- **Trevor:** 0 overrides — but that's because Trevor doesn't auto-schedule; you drag and it stays.
- **Motion AI:** 4 manual overrides across the same two weeks. Two were double-booking fixes after a meeting shifted, and two were cases where the AI moved a task into a time slot the user had marked as personal.

That result matches the user complaints on Reddit: Motion's AI is aggressive, and when a meeting shifts, the cascade can produce conflicts. The alternatives are either more conservative (Reclaim) or fully transparent (Trevor), which is why users report them as "calmer" tools.

## Who Should Look Elsewhere Entirely?

Motion's alternatives are great at scheduling, but they are not full project managers. Consider the right tool for the whole job:

- **If you need autonomous project management** (tasks, projects, sprints, and dependencies), look at Height or Motion's own project plan — scheduling alone won't cover it.
- **If you need a shared team calendar** with booking links, Google Calendar itself (or Calendly) may be all you need.
- **If you need meeting transcription and notes** as part of your workflow, pair any of these with a note-taking tool rather than expecting a scheduler to handle it.
- **If you run an agency with client deadlines**, Akiflow's inbox + task + calendar combination is the closest to Motion's scope, at half the price.
