# Gamma vs Beautiful.ai 2026: Which AI Presentation Tool Wins?

**Quick Answer: Gamma wins for versatility and price, turning a single prompt into presentations, documents, websites, and social posts, with a permanent free plan of 400 credits and paid tiers from about $8 to $15 per month billed annually. Beautiful.ai wins for pure deck polish, with Smart Slides that auto-align your content, locked brand controls, and team libraries, at about $12 per month billed annually. Gamma is the better default for most people in 2026; Beautiful.ai is the better choice when your presentation quality and brand consistency are non-negotiable.**

Gamma is an AI-powered visual communication platform that generates presentations, documents, websites, and social graphics from a text prompt, with an expert AI agent that adapts the output to your stated goal. Beautiful.ai is an AI presentation design tool that automates layout through Smart Slides, ensuring every slide stays aligned and on-brand while you focus on content. Both tools promise to remove the busywork from deck creation, but they attack the problem differently: Gamma generates whole decks from a prompt, Beautiful.ai polishes decks you build slide by slide.

## How Much Does Gamma Cost in 2026?

Gamma's pricing is credit-based and unusually generous at the free tier. The permanent Free plan gives you 400 one-time AI credits, enough for roughly ten full deck generations, with no credit card required. When you need more, Plus costs about $8 per month billed annually, or $10 monthly, and includes 1,000 credits per month with Gamma branding removed. Pro runs $15 per month billed annually, or $20 monthly, and adds 4,000 credits per month plus analytics and API access. Team plans start around $20 per seat per month. Gamma's own [pricing page](https://gamma.app), as of July 2026, confirms the structure: Free at $0 with 400 signup credits, Plus at $9 per seat per month, Pro at $18, and Ultra at $90 for heavy production teams. The free tier alone covers a student or a casual user indefinitely.

## How Much Does Beautiful.ai Cost in 2026?

Beautiful.ai takes a simpler subscription approach with no meaningful free generation tier. The Pro plan starts around $12 per month billed annually, or about $14.50 per month on the annual plan with monthly billing at $45 if you want to switch anytime. There is a 14-day free trial so you can test the Smart Slides workflow before paying, and the official [Beautiful.ai](https://www.beautiful.ai) product page walks through the design system and team features. After the trial you must subscribe to keep using the product. Team and Enterprise plans add shared brand libraries, collaboration, and analytics for larger organizations. The pricing is higher than Gamma's entry point and there is no permanent free tier, which matters for students, freelancers, and anyone who wants to build a deck occasionally without a subscription. For a solo consultant who makes one client deck a month, Beautiful.ai's annual $144 to $174 bill is the entire cost of the relationship.

## How Do the AI Generation Workflows Differ?

This is the core difference between the two tools. Gamma is prompt-to-product: you describe the goal, the audience, and the content, and the AI agent generates a complete deck, document, website, or social post in one pass, then you refine individual sections. The homepage puts it plainly: "Visual storytelling without limits," with an expert AI agent that knows your goals. Beautiful.ai is slide-by-slide assistance: you choose a template, add content, and the Smart Slides engine automatically reflows, aligns, and restyles your layout as you type. You build the deck yourself with AI handling the design, rather than asking AI to build the whole deck. Gamma is better when you have an idea and no slides; Beautiful.ai is better when you have the content and want guaranteed professional layout.

## Which Tool Produces Better Presentation Quality?

Independent comparisons in 2026 rate both tools at 4.7 out of 5 on G2, so raw quality is close, but the character of the output differs. Beautiful.ai decks look like corporate pitch decks from a design team: consistent spacing, aligned grids, and on-brand color schemes enforced by the platform. Gamma decks look modern and flexible, with more visual variety across themes, because the AI agent chooses layouts per section. Beautiful.ai's advantage is determinism: you cannot accidentally produce an ugly slide because the layout engine refuses to let it happen. Gamma's advantage is range: the same prompt can yield a presentation, a document, a website, or a social post, and the visual style adapts to the format. If you present to enterprise clients weekly, Beautiful.ai's consistency is the safer bet; if you create across formats, Gamma does more with less work.

## How Do Templates and Brand Controls Compare?

Beautiful.ai built its reputation on brand governance. Team plans give you brand libraries where logos, fonts, and color palettes are locked, so every deck created by every employee matches the official look, and the platform offers analytics on how presentations are used. That is a real feature for marketing teams and agencies, not a nice-to-have. Gamma is more permissive: templates and themes exist, but the AI agent generates freely and brand control is lighter, which suits startups and individuals who want speed over governance. The tradeoff shows up in the same project: a marketing team of eight people enforcing a visual identity across every deck is far better served by Beautiful.ai, while a founder who wants a deck and a landing page from one prompt gets more from Gamma. For solo consultants, the equation is simpler: a single designer can maintain their own brand discipline in either tool, and the choice falls to whether they need the governance or the range. ## What Can You Create Beyond Presentations?

Gamma's multi-format output is its strongest differentiator. The same AI agent that builds a deck also builds documents, websites, and social posts, and the platform markets itself as a visual communication platform rather than a presentation tool. A product launch can be one prompt that produces a pitch deck, a one-page site, and a social graphic set. Beautiful.ai is strictly a presentation tool: you get decks, and the platform optimizes everything around deck quality, templates, and sharing. That focus is why its decks look so consistent, but it also caps the tool's usefulness for people whose needs span formats. If you live inside one format, presentations, Beautiful.ai's focus is a feature. If your work spans formats, Gamma's range saves you from learning four different tools.

## What Are the Downsides of Each Tool?

Gamma's weaknesses are refinement and polish. AI-generated decks still need editing, and although the editor is solid, fine-grained control over every pixel is weaker than in dedicated design tools. Credits also gate your usage: heavy users who generate many decks a month hit the credit ceiling and pay more. Beautiful.ai's weaknesses are cost and rigidity. There is no permanent free tier, the annual commitment is steep for occasional users, and the Smart Slides engine, while consistent, can fight you when you want a layout it does not provide. The locked brand system is great for governance but frustrating for individual creators who want to experiment. Neither tool is a replacement for a designer in the room, but both eliminate the worst part of deck building, which is the layout grunt work.

## Should You Choose Gamma or Beautiful.ai in 2026?

Match the tool to the workflow, not to the hype. Choose Gamma if you want prompt-to-deck speed, multi-format output, or a free tier to start with, and your presentations are one of several content types you produce. Choose Beautiful.ai if your job is business decks specifically, your team needs enforced brand consistency, or you want the guarantee that no slide ever looks broken. Choose both if you have the budget: Gamma for fast drafts and non-presentation formats, Beautiful.ai for the final polished client-facing deck.
