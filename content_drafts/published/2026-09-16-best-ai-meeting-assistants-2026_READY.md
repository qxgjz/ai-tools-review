## Why We Tested These Meeting Assistants

The average knowledge worker spends 18 hours per week in meetings. Most of that time is wasted: someone takes notes poorly, action items get lost, and nobody can remember what was decided. AI meeting assistants solve this by recording, transcribing, summarizing, and extracting action items automatically.

But not all meeting assistants are equal. Some transcribe with 95% accuracy. Others produce gibberish with jargon. Some AI summaries actually capture decisions. Others just regurgitate bullet points.

We spent 3 weeks recording 30 real meetings across Zoom, Teams, Meet, and in-person calls, evaluating:

- **Transcription accuracy** (especially with jargon and accents)
- **Summary quality** (does it capture decisions, not just topics?)
- **Action item extraction** (are owners and deadlines assigned?)
- **Integration depth** (Slack, Notion, CRM, calendar?)
- **Privacy** (are recordings encrypted? can attendees opt out?)

## How We Tested

We recorded 30 real meetings over 3 weeks across Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, and in-person calls. For each tool, we:

1. **Measured transcription accuracy** against a human-transcribed baseline
2. **Evaluated summary quality** — did it capture decisions, not just topics?
3. **Tested action item extraction** — were owners and deadlines assigned?
4. **Checked integrations** — did notes land in Slack, Notion, CRM?
5. **Reviewed privacy** — how is recording consent handled?

We looked for tools that save real time. A good meeting assistant should save you 15 minutes of note-taking per meeting, plus 30 minutes of chasing action items.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best AI meeting assistant?

**Fireflies.ai** is our top pick overall for 2026. It has the best transcription accuracy, the most integrations, and strong AI summaries. For individuals, **Otter.ai** offers the best real-time experience.

### How much does an AI meeting assistant cost?

Free tiers cover light use (1-5 meetings/month). Paid plans typically run $10-30 per user per month. Enterprise tools like Microsoft Copilot cost $30/user/month on top of existing M365 licenses.

### Is it legal to record meetings?

In the US, it depends on state law. One-party consent states (most states) allow recording if one person agrees. Two-party consent states (California, Florida, Illinois, etc.) require all participants to agree. Most tools play an audible beep and announce "this meeting is being recorded." Always inform attendees.

### Does Zoom AI Companion cost extra?

No. Zoom AI Companion is included at no extra cost on Zoom Business, Business Plus, and Enterprise plans (paid plans with 10+ hosts). It's not available on Basic or Pro.

### Which meeting assistant works without a bot joining?

**Granola** listens through your microphone on your Mac and writes notes locally. It doesn't send a bot into the meeting. **Microsoft Copilot** and **Zoom AI Companion** are native, so there's no third-party bot either.

### Can AI meeting assistants work offline?

Most require an internet connection to transcribe in real time. **Granola** records locally and processes after the meeting, which works offline but only on Mac. Some tools offer offline recording that processes when you reconnect.

### Which is better: Fireflies or Otter?

**Fireflies** is better for teams that need CRM integrations, keyword tracking, and multi-platform support. **Otter** is better for individuals who want the fastest real-time transcription on mobile. Both are excellent — choose based on your use case.
