## Why We Tested These AI Support Tools

Customer support is one of the clearest AI use cases. Tickets are repetitive, knowledge bases exist, and the ROI of deflecting even 20% of conversations is obvious. But not every AI support tool delivers. Some "AI chatbots" still feel like 2015-era rule-based bots with a fresh coat of paint. Others genuinely understand context, pull from your docs, and hand off to humans gracefully.

We spent 3 weeks with a simulated support team of 6 agents, evaluating:

- **Resolution rate** — can the AI actually close tickets without a human?
- **Setup time** — how long to connect your knowledge base?
- **Handoff quality** — does the human agent see the full conversation?
- **Pricing** — per-resolution vs per-seat vs platform fee
- **Multilingual support** — for global teams

## How We Tested

We ran a 3-week evaluation with a simulated support team of 6 agents handling 200 test tickets across email, chat, and social. For each tool, we:

1. **Connected a 50-article knowledge base** and measured indexing quality
2. **Tested AI resolution rate** on 100 common questions
3. **Measured human handoff quality** (did the agent see the full conversation?)
4. **Evaluated setup time** (how long until first ticket resolved?)
5. **Compared pricing** for the AI-inclusive tier

We looked for tools that resolve real tickets, not just tools that chat. A 60% resolution rate saves a team of 10 agents roughly 4 FTE per year.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best AI customer support tool?

**Intercom Fin** is our top pick overall for 2026. It has the highest automatic resolution rate and the most polished human handoff. For smaller teams on a budget, **Freshdesk** offers the best value.

### How much does AI customer support cost?

Pricing varies widely. **Tidio** starts at $29/month flat for small stores. **Freshdesk** is $45/agent/month. **Zendesk** is $55-89/agent/month. **Intercom** charges per-resolution on top of platform fees. Budget $30-100 per agent per month for meaningful AI features.

### Can AI replace human support agents?

Not entirely. AI handles 40-60% of repetitive tickets (password resets, order status, FAQs), but humans are still needed for complex issues, empathy, and escalation. The best setup is AI deflection + human agents for the hard tickets — not AI replacing humans entirely.

### How does AI resolution work?

Modern AI support tools connect to your knowledge base, help center, and past tickets. When a new ticket comes in, the AI reads it, searches your knowledge, and either answers directly (if it's confident) or drafts a reply for a human to review. Confidence scores determine whether the AI responds alone.

### Which AI support tool is best for Shopify?

**Tidio** is the best choice for Shopify stores. It integrates natively with Shopify, pulls product and order data, and handles cart recovery, product questions, and order tracking out of the box.

### How long does setup take?

Simple AI chatbot setup takes 1-2 hours (connect your knowledge base, configure branding). Production-grade AI resolution with custom intents and routing takes 1-2 weeks. Plan for a 30-day trial to measure real deflection rates.

### Does Zendesk AI work on old accounts?

Yes, Zendesk AI is available on Suite Growth and above. If you're on an older legacy plan, you'll need to migrate to the new Suite model. Most customers see AI features activate within a day of upgrading.
