<h2>What is Midjourney?</h2>
<p>Midjourney is an independent research lab that produces an AI image generation program of the same name. Launched in open beta in July 2022, it quickly became the gold standard for AI-generated art due to its exceptional aesthetic quality and artistic range.</p>

<p>In 2026, Midjourney runs on its <strong>V7 model</strong>, which introduced several major improvements: enhanced photorealism, better human anatomy, video generation capabilities, 3D asset export (GLB format), and a redesigned web interface that no longer requires Discord. The tool now supports 50+ artistic styles, from photorealistic to anime, oil painting, cyberpunk, and watercolor.</p>
<p>Midjourney's unique approach uses a proprietary machine learning model trained on millions of images, with a focus on artistic composition and aesthetic appeal rather than literal text-to-image mapping. This is why Midjourney images tend to look more "artistic" and less "AI-generated" than competitors.</p>

<h2>What is DALL-E 3?</h2>
<p>DALL-E 3 is OpenAI's third-generation text-to-image model, released in October 2023 and significantly upgraded in 2025-2026 with GPT-4o integration. Unlike Midjourney's artistic focus, DALL-E 3 was designed from the ground up for <strong>prompt adherence</strong> and <strong>text rendering accuracy</strong>.</p>

<p>DALL-E 3's key differentiator is its ability to render <strong>accurate text within images</strong> — a capability that remains challenging for most AI image generators, including Midjourney.
