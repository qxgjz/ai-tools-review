## FAQ

**Which is better for beginners?**
Canva AI is significantly easier for beginners. Its drag-and-drop interface, template library, and all-in-one workflow mean you can produce professional graphics in minutes without any design experience. Firefly requires understanding of the Creative Cloud ecosystem and Photoshop for editing.

**Does Adobe Firefly come with Photoshop?**
No — Firefly is a separate subscription at $9.99/month. However, if you have a Creative Cloud plan (Photoshop single app $22.99/mo or All Apps $55/mo), Firefly generative credits are included. Check your plan details to confirm your credit allocation.

**Can I use AI-generated images commercially?**
Yes, both platforms grant commercial use rights on paid plans. Adobe Firefly has an advantage here — it's trained on licensed Adobe Stock content and public domain material, and Adobe offers IP indemnification for enterprise customers. Canva also grants commercial rights on Pro and Teams plans, but its training data sources are less transparent.

**Which has better free tier?**
For pure image generation, Firefly's free tier is more generous (unlimited standard generations + 25 premium credits). For designing and exporting finished graphics, Canva's free tier is better (50 credits + full editor + no watermarks on exports).

**Can Canva AI replace Photoshop?**
For social media graphics, presentations, and basic marketing materials — yes, for most users. For professional photo retouching, compositing, print design, and pixel-level editing — no, Photoshop is still significantly more powerful. The gap narrows each year but hasn't closed.

**Do either tools offer video generation?**
Adobe Firefly offers video generation via the Firefly Video Model (available on paid plans, uses premium credits). Canva does not offer native AI video generation as of September 2026, though it has video editing tools. For AI video, also consider Runway or Pika Labs.

**How many credits do I actually need?**
A casual user creating 5-10 images per month will be fine on either free tier. A social media manager producing 20-30 graphics per week will need Canva Pro (500 credits/mo) or Firefly Standard (2,000 premium credits/mo). A design team doing high-volume generation will need Firefly Pro (4,000 credits) or Canva Teams.
