## Detailed Tool Reviews

### Udio — Deep Dive

Udio was founded by former Google DeepMind researchers and launched in 2025. Its model uses a two-stage generation process: first composing the musical structure (chord progression, melody, rhythm), then rendering the audio with high-fidelity synthesis. In our testing, Udio produced tracks with better dynamic range than Suno — the quiet sections were actually quiet and the loud sections had punch without clipping.

The platform supports up to 4 minutes of generated audio per track, which is longer than Suno's 2-minute default (Suno Pro can extend to 4 minutes but requires extra credits). Udio also supports stem separation: you can export the vocals, drums, bass, and melody as separate audio files, which is invaluable for producers who want to remix AI-generated tracks.

Pricing: Free tier gives 10 credits per day. Premium ($10/month) gives 1,200 credits. Premium+ ($30/month) gives 6,000 credits and early access to new features.

### Soundraw — Deep Dive

Soundraw takes a compositional approach rather than a generative one. You select your genre, mood, tempo, and instrumentation, and the AI assembles a custom track from a vast library of musical phrases. You can then drag and drop sections to rearrange the structure, extend the track, or swap instruments in real time.

This non-destructive editing workflow is Soundraw's killer feature. If you're creating a YouTube video that needs background music to match specific scene changes, you can extend the chorus by 8 seconds or add a bridge without regenerating the entire track. No other AI music tool offers this level of compositional control.

Pricing: Creator ($16.99/month) includes unlimited generation and commercial rights. Indie ($42.99/month) adds stem export and priority support.

### Mubert — Deep Dive

Mubert specializes in generative ambient music that never repeats. Instead of generating discrete tracks, Mubert creates infinite audio streams tailored to your chosen mood. This makes it perfect for background music in streams, podcasts, or video games where you need continuous audio without obvious loops.

Mubert's library includes over 10 million royalty-free samples processed through its generative engine. The Artist plan ($14.90/month) includes unlimited track downloads and commercial rights. Mubert also offers an API for developers who want to integrate generative music into their own products.

### AIVA — Deep Dive

AIVA was launched in 2016, making it one of the oldest AI music platforms. It was originally designed to compose classical and cinematic scores, and its model excels at orchestral arrangements, piano sonatas, and film-score style compositions. AIVA is the only AI music tool that supports MIDI export, meaning you can take the generated composition into Ableton Live, Logic Pro, or FL Studio and edit every note.

The Pro plan ($33/month) includes 300 downloads, commercial rights, and MIDI export. AIVA also offers a free tier with 3 downloads per month for personal, non-commercial use.

### Beatoven.ai — Deep Dive

Beatoven.ai is built specifically for content creators who need background music for podcasts, YouTube videos, and social media content. It analyzes your video or audio file's pacing and mood, then generates matching background music that automatically adapts to scene changes.

The platform integrates directly with YouTube and podcast hosting platforms, making it easy to add background music to your existing content workflow. At $20/month, Beatoven.ai is more expensive than generic AI music generators but saves hours of music selection and editing time.

## Common Questions About AI Music

### Do AI music generators produce copyright-free tracks?

On paid plans, yes. All the tools reviewed above grant commercial usage rights on their paid tiers. This means you can use the generated music in monetized YouTube videos, podcasts, films, and commercial projects without additional licensing fees. Always review each platform's current terms as AI music copyright law is still evolving.

### Can I trademark songs I generate with AI music tools?

This varies by platform. Suno and Udio grant you commercial ownership of generated tracks on paid plans, which means you can register copyright for your specific composition. However, the underlying AI model and training data remain the platform's intellectual property. Consult a legal professional for trademark and copyright questions specific to your use case.

### What audio quality do AI music tools produce?

Most tools output 44.1kHz WAV files or 320kbps MP3 files, which is broadcast quality. The generative quality has improved dramatically in 2025-2026, with leading tools like Suno and Udio producing tracks that are indistinguishable from human-produced music to the average listener.
