## Why Look for a Stable Diffusion Alternative?

Stable Diffusion is open-source and free, but it has real pain points:

1. **Hardware barrier.** A good local setup needs 12GB+ VRAM. Most laptops can't run it well.
2. **Steep prompt learning.** Mastery takes weeks — samplers, CFG scales, negative embeddings.
3. **Model sprawl.** You have to hunt for checkpoints on Civitai. Version mismatches are common.
4. **No built-in upscaling or editing.** You're stitching together ControlNet, inpainting, and upscalers manually.

If any of that sounds familiar, one of these 7 tools will save you hours.
