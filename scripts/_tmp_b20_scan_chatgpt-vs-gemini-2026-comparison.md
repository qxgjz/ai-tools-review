**Q: Which is better for coding, ChatGPT or Gemini?**
A: ChatGPT is generally better for coding, especially for complex tasks, debugging, and understanding existing codebases. Gemini is good but lags slightly behind in code quality and edge case handling.

**Q: Can I use both ChatGPT and Gemini for free?**
A: Yes! Both offer free tiers. Gemini's free tier is significantly more capable, offering Gemini 2.5 Flash with generous limits, Google Workspace integration, and image generation. ChatGPT's free tier uses GPT-4o mini with more limited features.

**Q: Which has better image generation?**
A: ChatGPT uses DALL-E 3, which is excellent for photorealistic and artistic images. Gemini uses Imagen 3, which is also very strong, especially for text rendering in images. Both are top-tier; the choice depends on your specific style preferences.

**Q: Is Gemini better than ChatGPT for Google Workspace?**
A: Absolutely. Gemini has native, deep integration with Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Drive, Calendar, and Meet. ChatGPT can work with Google Workspace through plugins, but the experience is not as seamless or powerful.

**Q: Which is more accurate for factual information?**
A: Gemini generally has an edge for factual queries because it's powered by Google Search and has better real-time information retrieval. ChatGPT's Browse with Bing is good but not as comprehensive as Google Search.

**Q: Can I cancel my subscription anytime?**
A: Yes, both ChatGPT Plus and Gemini Advanced offer month-to-month subscriptions that can be cancelled at any time. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

**Q: Which is better for students?**

**Q: Do either of these tools support API access?**
