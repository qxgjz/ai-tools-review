3. ChatGPT automatically refines your prompt
4. Get 2-4 image variations
5. Ask ChatGPT to make specific changes ("make it brighter", "change the background")
6. Download final image

**Pros:** Simple, intuitive, ChatGPT helps with prompts, conversational refinement
**Cons:** Less direct control, fewer parameters, slower iteration
